# VELDRYN 1.0.8 — Shape Your Adventure

**Status:** Expanded, unpublished release-copy concept prepared 11 November 2026. This file does **not** publish the update, change the required client version, deploy backend services, or enable release-gated systems.

**Basis:** Player-facing work completed or staged for the 1.0.8 candidate after the published 1.0.7 announcement on 7 November 2026. Final copy must be reduced to features verified in the production Android build and its matching backend deployment.

## Release concept

**Theme:** Veldryn feels more like a connected adventure: battles play out through real strikes, regions have clearer journeys, Dungeons reward preparation and counterplay, and long-term gear, Companion, Exploration, Faith, Guild, and profile progression is easier to understand.

**Player promise:** Your build and choices should be visible in play. Attack, Defense, equipment, buffs, Companions, tactics, regional preparation, and route decisions now have clearer and more consistent effects, while progression remains preserved for existing characters.

## In-game What's New

**Title:** Shape Your Adventure

1. **Combat Reforged:** Hunts now resolve hit by hit with real damage, misses, critical strikes, enemy HP, Attack, Defense, accuracy, evasion, and haste.
2. **Regional Dungeons:** Prepare for clearer level and Combat Power requirements, regional tiers, two possible final bosses, stronger encounter variety, and meaningful Companion counterplay.
3. **Explore a Bigger World:** Navigate Veldryn by story chapter, survey four Sunscar areas, journey through four Sunscar and Frostmarch zones, and unlock permanent Trailcraft bonuses.
4. **Forge Your Build:** Craft the complete Tier 5 equipment range, compare class-focused stat identities, refine gems for more XP, and manage equipment through clearer Forge, selling, and Salvage flows.
5. **Grow Your Collection:** Progress Combat Companions, discover pets and account-wide collection bonuses, earn secure profile badges, and personalize how your character appears.
6. **Guilds, Faith & Quests:** Take on rotating Guild activities, use clearer shared funding tools, choose from an expanded set of Faith blessings, and complete unified daily, weekly, and monthly objectives.

## Google Play — What's New

Combat now plays out hit by hit, with Attack, Defense, enemy HP, misses and critical strikes shaping every hunt.

Explore clearer regional journeys, improved Dungeons with Combat Power tiers and Companion counterplay, and new Sunscar surveys with permanent Trailcraft bonuses.

Craft the complete Tier 5 equipment range, enjoy better Forge and Salvage tools, expanded Faith and Quest goals, Guild improvements, pets, badges and profile polish.

## Detailed Release Notes

### Combat, balance and progression

- Ordinary hunts use deterministic real strikes rather than a cosmetic progress timer. Enemy HP falls from actual attacks, while misses, critical hits, attack cadence and incoming hits remain synchronized across live play, previews and offline settlement.
- Enemy Attack and Defense now contribute to real outgoing and incoming damage. Frostmarch and Ashlands candidate hunts also use damage-driven encounters rather than authored kill timers.
- Character stats, Combat Power, comparisons, readiness and combat presentation share the same effective-stat path. Permanent Combat Power is applied once, avoiding duplicated Attack scaling or unintended Defense from offensive bonuses.
- Companion contribution is visible in Combat Power but remains bounded so a Companion supports rather than replaces the character build.
- All nine classes retain clearer Attack/Defense equipment identities without deleting existing gear, levels, set bonuses or character progress.
- Combat level thresholds were rebased around the longer progression curve while preserving earned XP, effective power, profession levels and legacy-save eligibility. Some displayed character levels and content requirements may therefore be lower than their old equivalents.
- Live hunt presentation includes class- and enemy-aware narration, wounded/finishing states, real HP progress and clearer recovery feedback.
- Prepared late-region builds remain inside their authored provisioning targets after pets and combat buffs; underprepared equipment continues to create additional time and sustain pressure.

### Dungeons and co-op

- Dungeon browsing is organized by region, with clearer tier presentation, reward hints, route progress and regional atmosphere. Ashlands remains visibly **In Development** until its region release.
- New dungeon runs use explicit character-level and Combat Power gates. Quick Match, Live parties, Q-Mode, saved Echoes and NPC replacements all validate against the same regional requirements.
- Regional queues are consolidated and can select between two final bosses. Boss identity and private combat details remain hidden until the run reaches the appropriate reveal point.
- Asterfall, Sunscar and Frostmarch tier pressure was calibrated with deterministic multi-run clear-rate checks, including separate final-boss corrections rather than one global difficulty multiplier.
- Encounters have more distinct archetypes and real mechanics: support enemies, focused attacks, damage-over-time pressure, barriers, interrupts, healing pressure, execution attacks and elite modifiers.
- Route choices include genuine utility, risk, elite and combat alternatives. Live route decisions remain server-authoritative and cannot bypass voting.
- Echo boon choices, artifacts, curses, event rooms and risk rooms apply real server-simulated effects while preserving the run reward budget.
- Equipped Combat Companions can counter specific revealed mechanics. Examples include real Wisp interrupts and bounded offensive, defensive or recovery responses; a counter modifies the existing assist rather than adding an extra party member.
- Dungeon preparation provides clearer Companion selection, matchup guidance, invalid-loadout recovery and cache-first browsing.
- Frostfall is prepared with five event-only difficulty tiers, scaled access and rewards, and themed Guild/Dungeon presentation for its scheduled event window.

### World and Exploration

- The World screen is organized around chapter journeys and scenic current-location cards. Dungeons have their own destination instead of being mixed into World navigation.
- Sunscar and Frostmarch each provide four playable zones with clearer combat, gathering, scouting and story progression.
- Exploration includes four repeatable Sunscar area surveys with gradual skill requirements.
- Every ten Exploration levels grants a Trailcraft step, up to +10% gathering speed and +20% scouting speed at level 100.
- Combat awards 30% more passive Exploration XP than before, while active scouting remains the fastest route-training method.
- Current-region routes are prioritized and other regions sit behind an accessible disclosure, reducing long scrolling and accidental cross-region choices.
- Ashlands remains unreleased and is presented as In Development; legacy saves located there are returned safely to the nearest released region without losing progress.

### Equipment, Forge and professions

- The full Tier 5 equipment collection is released for crafting, with complete class/slot artwork and the existing two-path class build structure.
- Equipment stats better express each class role while conserving the shared item budget. Character sheets and item comparisons show the same effective values used by combat.
- The Forge emphasizes collection progress and exact crafted copies, with clearer rarity, quality, set, enhancement and socket information.
- Equipment crafting supports timed active slots and a protected waiting backlog, including safe cancellation and prerequisite preparation.
- Direct and bulk selling and Salvage support Inventory, Bank/Forge ownership and the Equipment Pouch with locked/favorite/equipped protections.
- Salvage returns bounded ordinary Forge materials and previews the actual recovery for each exact equipment copy.
- Stat and Effect Gems use separate sockets. Rolled gem bonuses, Grindstone-style rerolls, extraction safeguards and actual-value previews are clearer.
- Gem refinement grants 20 additional Enchanting XP per refinement grade.
- T2–T4 equipment crafting grants 25% more profession XP; Tier 5 uses its authored release values.
- Gathering-tool blueprints are rarer, tool crafting requires a more meaningful material/Gold investment, and the added Smithing XP better reflects that cost.
- Cooking, Alchemy and Faith support faster one-tap activity switching, clearer detail disclosures and more reliable continuous activity settlement.
- Food healing is 15% lower, keeping preparation and regional sustain relevant without changing food identities.

### Companions, pets and collections

- Combat Companions have clearer acquisition paths, progression, role identity and use across hunts and Dungeons.
- Companion Training appears as related progression in Skills, supports direct earned-XP claims, and explains that it improves Companions rather than the character's base stats.
- Companion Mastery and bounded assist contribution improve long-term value without allowing a Companion to dominate the owner's combat output.
- The collection roster includes 42 remastered pets and expanded regional/event acquisition sources.
- Owned and selected collectibles use one capped, account-wide bonus path. Collection effects are visible without allowing unlimited stacking as more pets and profile cosmetics are added.
- Pet, background and border bonuses are shared across characters on the account; selecting a cosmetic does not duplicate its passive ownership bonus.

### Faith, Quests and long-term goals

- Faith expands to 22 blessings, adding combat-tempo tiers and Festival Favor alongside combat, HP, gathering, production and XP choices.
- Festival Favor grants +10% common event currency while active; it does not multiply fixed or prestige event rewards.
- Holy Water remains the Faith resource, with lower late-game drop rates and whole-percent player-facing odds.
- Faith practice XP is 20% lower across tiers to support the longer progression curve, while continuous practice and reservation progress are more reliable.
- Daily, weekly and monthly repeatable objectives use shared 30 / 150 / 450 action targets and no longer present the daily board as class-specific.
- Completed Faith practices can advance eligible repeatable objectives.
- Contract reward receipts no longer leave persistent already-settled notifications.
- The Asterfall story finale rewards the permanent **Oathbreaker** account title and Oathglass materials instead of a retired cape; completed legacy characters receive the title safely.

### Guilds and social play

- Guild Projects support a standard weekly project plus a voted optional challenge, with clearer difficulty, contribution and reward presentation.
- Rotating Guild Bosses provide Tank-, Damage- and Support-focused encounters, original boss artwork and clearer contribution feedback.
- Guild Home, Hall, Construction, Muster, Bank and Project screens use a more compact hierarchy with clearer next actions.
- Guild Bank Gold and released materials can fund eligible Construction and Guild Skill upgrades. Needed-now and upcoming resources are separated, and permanent deposits remain explicit.
- Guild Bank deposits accept released skill resources and allow sensible prefunding for published upcoming requirements.
- Guild Muster status, Rally progress, member contributions and claim-ready states are easier to understand.
- Party Contracts show member contributions and claim readiness.
- World Chat currently shows English and Spanish public channels while retaining the underlying channel records for future language rollout.

### Profiles, identity and quality of life

- Six collectible player badges have secure server-verified unlocks. Staff identity remains automatic, while one collectible or Supporter-tenure badge can be equipped.
- Profile customization separates badges from Name Style and provides a dedicated badge gallery with source and ownership information.
- Character/Profile, Combat Power, Guild, Dungeon, Companion, Faith and activity guidance received expanded localization and safer fallbacks.
- Account-linking and Google services presentation is clearer, with improved pending-action, recovery and duplicate-submit protection.
- Inventory, crafting, gathering and event screens use larger labeled disclosures instead of difficult-to-tap miniature chevrons.
- Equipment and resource inspections provide more useful sources, uses, comparison details and next actions.
- The Community Roadmap allows up to three reversible, account-scoped votes and clearly separates proposed ideas from released features.

## Explicitly not part of the 1.0.8 launch claim

- **Player Market:** pricing, custody, escrow, concurrency and affordability work remains hidden and release-gated. There is no live trading UI or item transfer service.
- **World Bosses:** deterministic combat, verified rosters, War Camp, World Impact and atomic settlement work remains a disabled prototype/reference implementation.
- **Dungeon Marks Shop:** catalogue, pricing, UI and transactional foundations remain hard-off until art, production RPC and release-gate verification are complete.
- **Party Weekly Events and Party leaderboards:** foundations and simulations remain disabled.
- **Arena and public Rankings:** both remain release-gated.
- **Ashlands:** world and Dungeon previews remain In Development.
- Community Roadmap entries describe proposals; a vote count or roadmap stage does not mean the feature is playable.
