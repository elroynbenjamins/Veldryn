## Release concept

**Theme:** Character progression, co-op, social play and presentation have all moved forward: stronger equipment crafting, deeper companion growth, clearer regional Dungeons, richer story exploration and smoother everyday play.

**Player promise:** Your choices and progress should feel more meaningful: build distinct gear, grow companions, enter the right Dungeon tier, follow the story and move smoothly between quests, crafting, hunts, Guild and profile screens.

## Detailed Release Notes

### Equipment and economy

- Tier 5 base Gold repriced from 3,500 to 6,000, producing monotonic full-set costs: T4 (~136k Smithing / ~118k Tailoring) < T5 (~195k) < T6 (~340k).
- T5 material quantities rebalanced so that Smithing and Tailoring both require more regional materials than T4, using the 1.25× tier material multiplier.
- Every T5 piece requires one Sunfused Fixative (two for affix slots), preserving the Scorpion Venom bottleneck as a meaningful tier gate.
- T5 farm-time bands widened to 0.75–6.2 hours per piece to accommodate the affix material multiplier while remaining below T6 floors.

### T5 forged affixes

- Three of ten T5 equipment slots carry a secondary combat stat: Gloves (Haste), Amulet (Crit Rate), Boots (Evasion).
- Affix percentage scales with crafted rarity and quality: Common/low-quality copies receive 2%, Epic baseline 3%, strong Rare/Epic copies 4%, and exceptional Legendary/Mythic results reach 5%.
- Affix slots cost 15% more regional materials, 10% more crafting time, one extra Sunfused Fixative, and award 20% more profession XP.
- Affix stats flow through the full combat pipeline: equipment build stats, combat modifiers (speed, incoming damage, expected crit) and effective stats all reflect equipped T5 affixes.
- Item inspection displays the affix stat, percentage, rarity and quality on equipped T5 pieces.

### Combat and hunt presentation

- Hunt strike presentation upgraded with new attack sprites, impact burst effects and critical-hit visuals that adapt to class and enemy.
- Hunting preview expanded with richer stat previews and preparation information.
- Combat presentation logic refined for clearer wounded/finishing state transitions and narration.

### Gathering and pixel scenes

- New pixel-art gathering scene system with animated regional backgrounds for caves, forests and waterways across Asterfall, Sunscar, Frostmarch and Ashlands.
- Material-accurate ore chunks, timber logs and gathering character sprites (mining, chopping, fishing cast/wait/pull) at 1×/2×/3× density.
- Sprite animation framework with frame-based playback, motion easing and composable sprite layers.
- Gathering scene preview added to the development browser review tool.

### Profiles and identity

- Profile badge showcase expanded with new settings UI, gallery and ownership information.
- Profile wardrobe chips redesigned for clearer cosmetic selection.
- Player styled names refined with improved rendering and fallback behavior.
- Public profile scene and profile stage polish: updated layout, scene preview and showcase panel.
- Profile badge showcase v3 migration prepared.

### Dungeon visual polish

- Live vote countdown shows urgent tint and pulse animation as time runs out.
- Route decision cards display a proportional vote meter under each choice.
- Boss telegraph events show a phase-threshold bar with a pulsing dot indicator.
- Dungeon shop offers display category glyphs and affordability status.
- Loadout preparation shows tier pips and colored stat deltas.
- Victory moment celebration includes a radial halo and sparkle burst.
- Co-op dungeon browser received minor layout improvements.

### Other improvements

- Home activity focus updated with additional context.
- World chapter navigator layout refinement.
- Smithing collection display improvements for clarity and density.
- Combat Power companion attribution capped at 18%, preventing Companion from dominating the build rating.
- Equipment tier memory system for tracking per-tier crafting context.
- Social notification state v1 migration prepared.
- Tool blueprint probabilities finalized at 3.5%, 1.4%, 0.7% tiers with a 0.10% floor; tool crafting awards up to +75% additional Smithing XP.
- Reinforced Timber and Bannerweave Cloth profession unlocks lowered to level 4.

### Story, contracts and solo combat

- The Asterfall Journal now includes a spoiler-safe Chronicle, revealed after the relevant story rewards are claimed, with chapter recaps and a clearer handoff into Sunscar.
- Eligible Contract Board hunts and gathering tasks can automatically travel to the required unlocked location and start the activity; locked or ineligible activities retain their checks.
- Normal encounter-search times are reduced by two seconds in all regions, preserving a positive search duration.
- Tank and Support solo hunting has been calibrated closer to Damage-class clear speeds without changing Dungeon or Boss role output.

### Player profiles, Guilds and social

- Public player profiles can display the selected character's ten equipped gear slots above Achievements, with rarity-aware visuals, safe item inspection and privacy controls.
- Equipped identity badges and up to three intentionally featured profile badges remain independent; empty showcase slots do not automatically expose other badges.
- Guild member rosters show last-active information, and Guild Chat history now displays up to 50 messages.
- The permanent Founder badge redemption flow includes the `DiscordFounder` code, limited to one redemption per account and expiring on 5 December 2026. Verify that live redemption is enabled before using this in published promotion.

### Reliability and quality of life

- Dungeon group-finding eligibility now checks actual Combat Power and published Asterfall/Sunscar tier requirements, fixing blocked eligible LFG entries.
- Chat automatically keeps the latest messages visible above the mobile keyboard.
- Character deletion confirmation can open the Android keyboard correctly when prerequisite safety checks are pending.
- Switching to an existing character in a previously visited region no longer incorrectly triggers the locked-region travel gate; the actual storyline travel gate remains enforced.
- Expanded automated Android, mobile UI and Dungeon regression checks support release validation.

### Regional Dungeons, party formation and matchmaking

- The currently published regular Dungeon entries in `main` are **Rootbound Vault (Asterfall)** and **Buried Observatory (Sunscar)**. Other authored historical and future Expedition definitions do not automatically imply released queue access.
- Asterfall begins at **character level 10**, with three Combat Power tier requirements of **2,500 / 3,750 / 5,000**.
- Sunscar begins at **character level 25**, with three Combat Power tier requirements of **6,000 / 7,250 / 8,500** and the additional **Fallen Knight Chapter I clear** requirement.
- New regular runs publish only difficulty **Tiers I–III**. Old saved Tier IV/V run and reward history remains recoverable rather than being treated as new playable queues.
- Quick Match, Live, Q-Mode and LFG are expected to apply server-owned entry restrictions; the latest LFG fix uses actual character-sheet Combat Power rather than an implicit zero or obsolete level-only tier calculation.
- The Dungeon browser uses a character-scoped cached catalog to speed reopening, without caching or trusting live loadouts, matchmaking or reward state.
- Dungeon preparation includes an owned-Companion selector, role and availability checks, and server-derived potential encounter-counter hints. Select companion bonuses can change specific real room outcomes without adding extra party members.
- Difficulty selection contains a verified rewards/drop-information panel. Do not suggest guaranteed equipment/material drops or display fabricated loot probabilities where no published table exists.
- Route votes, urgency timers, event choices, boss warnings, loadout readability and victory presentation have been visually refined.

### Companion progression and balancing

- **Companion Mastery** is a separate account-wide skill from levels **1–100**. Training Ground claims, completed Expeditions and victorious Trial floors can award Mastery XP through validated reward events.
- At Mastery level 100, companion combat HP, Attack and Defense bonuses reach **+5%**; companion XP reaches **+10%**, and the relative strength of equipped companion assists reaches **+5%**. These are Companion-specific bonuses, not direct character-base-stat bonuses.
- The Training Ground now shows its own facility level and XP claim availability, with clearer 24-hour and seven-day rules.
- Companion contribution to the displayed Combat Power rating was brought to an **18% cap**, with corresponding owner power reattribution so the intended overall rating is preserved.
- Expedition eligibility, companion availability, Dungeon pre-run selection and matching counters have clearer requirements and feedback.
- New Unlocks now shows canonical pet names rather than the redundant "Cosmetic Pet" category line.

### Crafting, tool blueprints, potions and food

- The full Tier 5 equipment set has associated art and available crafting definitions, with material and Gold costs adjusted to progress sensibly after T4 and before T6.
- Standardize equipment rarity/quality scaling across all tiers and show the exact forged copy's stats, gems, enhancement and comparison values in inspection.
- Gathering-tool blueprints have approximately **25% lower relative monster-drop chances** than before; final published chance tiers are rounded to **3.5%, 1.4% and 0.7%**.
- Tool creation takes more materials and Gold, with increased Smithing XP to better reward the investment.
- **Bannerweave Cloth** and **Reinforced Timber** become available at their respective Tailoring/Smithing level **4** unlocks.
- Recipe and material requirements were adjusted across mid-tier equipment; Guild and general economic changes are tested against equipment progression.
- Cooking and Alchemy recipes, and Faith blessings, support direct one-tap activity switching with safer resource reservation handling.
- Food restores **15% less HP** than before. The change applies consistently to eating, auto-eat and combat sustain, without silently altering all Alchemy potion heals.
- Alchemy potion requirements, reservation behavior and boss/Dungeon use received balance and UI refinements; publish exact potion prices only against the production-matched catalog.

### Quests, Faith and player activities

- Repeatable activity goals now use **30 daily / 150 weekly / 450 monthly actions**, with common objective pools rather than class-specific quest board separation.
- Faith practice can contribute to qualifying repeatable quest objectives, alongside other common activities.
- Faith practice sessions reserve and repeat correctly instead of unexpectedly ending after one practice, and switching Faith tiers releases unused reservations safely.
- The Faith blessing system includes expanded permanent and temporary choices, with clearer progression and effects.
- Contract Board entries can start eligible hunts or gathering activities directly, traveling to the required unlocked area first.
- Party Contracts display accepted contributions from active members, claim-ready entitlements and notifications, subject to the corresponding server migration and deployment.
- Additional Quest Journal progress, reward inspection and localization improvements make objectives and claims easier to follow.

### Guilds, social progression and player identity

- Guild Projects emphasize the weekly choice, current progress, pending claims and the top contributors; additional Guild Hall, Bank, Construction, Muster, Raid and Shop screens have compact mobile-friendly layouts.
- Guild Choice follows a weekly vote/rollover flow, with active-week selection and a reminder when members have not voted.
- Guild first-join guidance and its replayable Guide Library cover the core systems, including localized instructions, accessible modal scrolling and clearer resource-funding explanations.
- Member rosters expose last-active labels and an inactive-first view. Guild Chat keeps **50 recent messages**.
- Party contract contributions and ready-to-claim indicators are more visible; permission checks protect other players' data.
- Profiles feature a read-only ten-slot equipped-gear view above Achievements for a selected public character, including safe socket and rarity information.
- Featured profile badges and the one name-adjacent worn badge remain independent, with explicit featured selection rather than auto-filled shelf items.
- Supporter badges support cumulative verified tenure at **0, 3, 6 and 12 months**, subject to the approved data migration and rollout.
- The **DiscordFounder** redemption flow is coded for a permanent badge, one redemption per account, with a **5 December 2026** expiry. Confirm live redemption and migration state before promoting the code.

### World, Journal, visuals and everyday usability

- The Asterfall Journal includes **six claim-gated Chronicle lore discoveries**, three-act progress and a clearer link into Sunscar after completing the chapter.
- The compact World encounter view now displays baseline kills/hour, XP/hour and Gold/hour, with more restrained enemy selection and loot details.
- Pixel gathering scenes, layered combat strike effects and Dungeon feedback add more readable, region-aware motion.
- Account > New Unlocks shows real pet names for both newly earned and older saved pet entries.
- Chat keeps recent messages visible above the keyboard; Android character deletion confirmation accepts text reliably; saved-character switching no longer incorrectly applies a new-region travel gate.
- Reward-claim recovery replays interrupted server commands using the original request identity rather than issuing duplicate claims.
- Release verification now includes additional core, UX, native Android build, Dungeon runtime and Android-emulator startup jobs. Passing static checks is not equivalent to an authenticated production gameplay test.
