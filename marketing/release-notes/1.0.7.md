## Detailed Release Notes

### Faith rework — PR #872
- Expand from 9 combat-only blessings to **18**, organized into **Combat, Gathering, Production, and Experience**.
- Add Gathering and Production Speed bonuses of +2%, +4%, and +6% at progressively higher Faith levels.
- Add +2%, +4%, and +6% Combat and profession XP blessings. **Faith practice XP does not receive this bonus.**
- Preserve existing combat blessing identifiers, favorites, training requirements, and Holy Water.
- Correct maximum-HP blessings to increase actual character HP rather than providing an unintended combat-power effect.
- Improve the blessing screen with grouped options and stronger-unlocked-first ordering.

### Repeatable Quest rewards — PR #874
- Replace some generic ore and material caches from daily, weekly, and monthly repeatable Quests with smaller, useful **Companion Essence** rewards or **Tempering Dust, Tempering Cores, and Asterfall Mineral Essence**.
- Companion Essence is credited directly to the account; equipment materials follow regular inventory and bank delivery rules.
- Update quest previews and claimed-reward receipts to show the correct resources.
- Preserve existing objectives, Gold, XP, and story quest rewards.

### Guild Projects and economy — PR #873
- Players joining a guild after a weekly project starts can contribute from the time they join. Past activity is not retroactively credited and completed projects do not pay new joiners retroactively.
- A **new-generation weekly project** rewards **2,500 Gold per qualifying member into the Guild Bank**, not each player's wallet. Existing v1/v2 weekly projects retain their promised **500 personal Gold** reward.
- Add Guild Bank Gold requirements alongside material costs for Guild Construction and Guild Skill upgrades.
- Improve project reward explanations, treasury ledger information, and localized descriptions.
