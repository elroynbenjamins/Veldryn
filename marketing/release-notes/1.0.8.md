## Detailed Release Notes

### Community Roadmap — PR #939
- A single **Community Roadmap** button appears in Account above What's New; it opens a separate voting screen.
- Vote on six *proposed* major expansions: Party Events, Guild PvP, Guild PvE, Arena, Leaderboards and World Bosses.
- Three active votes per account, reversible and server-validated, with vote counts and Consideration / Planned / In Development / Released stages.
- Voting on an idea is feedback, **not** an announcement that its underlying feature has launched.

### Guild activities — PRs #938, #941, #942
- A guaranteed standard weekly Guild Project plus a voted optional project, with rotating Combat/Easy, Skilling/Medium and Mixed/Hard candidates.
- Role-focused weekly Guild Boss rotation: Fallen Oathbreaker (Tank), Granite Moss Troll (Damage) and Veilbound Revenant (Support).
- One active weekly boss appears at a time; previous eligible claims remain accessible.
- Three optimized original boss portraits, updated combat visuals and clearer contribution feedback.

### Dungeons — PRs #913, #921, #929, #943, #944
- More distinct early combat formations, elite mechanics and enemy archetypes, including genuine support, damage-over-time, focused attacks, and warding.
- More meaningful room selection: new runs offer two combat paths and one randomized utility option at the second stage, while keeping five rooms and a final boss.
- Elite-only Thorned, Volatile, Warded, Swift and Attrition modifiers affect actual server-simulated battles.
- Interactive Echo boon selections provide real run effects; quicker Echo result settlement and more compact navigation.
- Live route choices require server-authoritative voting instead of allowing a direct selection bypass.
- Updated events, artifacts, curses, and risk rooms have server-calculated consequences; reward budgets are preserved.

### Class balance — PR #940
- All nine classes have distinct Attack/Defense emphasis from equipment that scales with gear.
- Character statistics, comparisons and server-validated combat consume the same effective stat system.
- Retains existing gear, saved characters, levels and set bonuses while refining class identity.

### World, equipment and quality-of-life — PRs #919, #925, #915, #906, #937, #935 and others
- Sunscar and Frostmarch reorganized into four playable zones each, with combat/gathering navigation improvements.
- Safer direct and bulk selling and salvaging of equipment, with protections and a material preview.
- Better equipment and resource inspections, Alchemy recipe selection and brewing controls.
- Improved Guild, Dungeon, Faith and Companion reward eligibility messaging, localization and UI polish.

## Release gates — DO NOT publish before these are satisfied

1. Confirm production database migrations for **Community Roadmap** (#939) and **Guild weekly projects and raids** (#938, #942) are applied in the correct order, or omit these features from release copy.
2. Rebuild, verify and deploy compatible gameplay and coop Supabase Edge functions. The shared-engine/committed-bundle mismatch was observed in CI; source-only changes do not establish live availability.
3. Verify fresh and legacy dungeon routes, Q-Mode and Live voting, combat replay, encounter outcomes, and reward claiming on staging and production.
4. Confirm the nine-class balance, Guild Boss rotation and voted weekly Guild Project behavior on current client/server versions.
5. Verify 1.0.8 Android release build and actual Play versionCode. Keep 1.0.7 as the current shipped/required version until a compatible 1.0.8 build is live.
6. Publish the production `app_release_notes` record only after approval and rollout. Until then keep `is_published=false` and its future placeholder timestamp.
