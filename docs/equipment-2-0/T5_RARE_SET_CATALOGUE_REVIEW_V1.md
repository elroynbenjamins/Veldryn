# Equipment 2.0 — rare T5 set catalogue review v1

**23 November 2026 · HIDDEN REVIEW ONLY · no runtime activation or main merge**

The approved Citadel and Bloodsteel visuals were stronger than the intended
T4 band. They are promoted into two Frostmarch T5 rare-set identities. Their
former T4 draft identities are retired and must never be registered, minted,
crafted, assigned art, or exposed in the Player Market.

## Rare-set rules

- Rare-set scarcity is independent from an item's rolled rarity and quality.
- Every member has one deterministic authored innate percentage stat.
- Innate stats stack with legal random affixes but are never duplicated or
  rarity-scaled a second time.
- Crafting requires Smithing 48 as a candidate gate; combat level is never
  required to craft. Candidate wear level is 56.
- Difficulty comes from a master blueprint, 35% additional refined/common
  materials, 45% additional Gold, 35% additional queue time, and tradeable
  rare combat signatures. Recipes never fail.
- Every source and quantity remains blocked until real payout telemetry,
  source registration, economy approval, escrow, commissions, and rollback
  safety are proven.

## Frostmarch Citadel — six-piece rare tank set

| Piece | Innate authored stat |
| --- | ---: |
| Helm | +3.0% maximum HP |
| Chest | +3.5% Defense |
| Legs | +3.0 percentage points Tenacity |
| Boots | +2.5% Defense |
| Mantle | +1.5 percentage points Ward |
| Ring | +2.5% maximum HP |

- **2 pieces — Citadel Foundation:** +4% maximum HP and +3% Defense.
- **4 pieces — Tower Guard:** a hit dealing at least 8% maximum HP after
  mitigation grants a 6%-maximum-HP shield for 6 seconds; 18-second internal
  cooldown.
- **6 pieces — Citadel Unbroken:** crossing below 35% HP grants 10% incoming
  damage reduction and 20% increased healing received for 8 seconds;
  35-second internal cooldown.

## Frostmarch Bloodsteel — four-piece rare aggressive-tank set

| Piece | Innate authored stat |
| --- | ---: |
| Gloves | +3.0% Attack |
| Boots | +2.8% Defense |
| Pendant | +3.0% direct class ability damage |
| Ring | +2.5% maximum HP |

- **2 pieces — Tempered Guard:** +3% Defense and +3% Attack.
- **4 pieces — Red Reprisal:** after eligible post-mitigation HP damage, the
  next basic attack within 5 seconds deals 18% increased damage and heals

  self-damage, and proc loops cannot trigger it.
