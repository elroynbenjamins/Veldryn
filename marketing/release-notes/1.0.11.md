# VELDRYN 1.0.11 — Stronger Every Day

**Status:** Unpublished concept draft · 19 November 2026 · not approved for rollout.
**Baseline:** Veldryn 1.0.10 is listed on Google Play (indexed update date 17 November 2026); public app rollout is separate from GitHub source merges.
**Theme:** More meaningful daily rewards and progression, smoother combat information, and more personality throughout the adventure.
**Scope rule:** The eight highlights below are **candidates**, not statements of shipped 1.0.11 features. Confirm exact release binary + production backend availability before promoting any of them.

## In-game What's New — proposed eight highlights

**Title:** Stronger Every Day

1. **Supply Journey:** Explore redesigned Daily Supplies, account-wide boosts, selectable milestone Blessings and a compact Boost Vault.
2. **Companion XP Banking:** Keep earning Companion XP at an Ascension level cap and use that stored XP when the cap increases.
3. **Cooking & Meals:** See Cooking XP for every batch, with stronger special-meal bonuses and clearer activation details.
4. **More Challenges:** Get one extra Daily, Weekly and Monthly Class Challenge, plus easier quest navigation and reward alerts.
5. **Crafting Progression:** Reach Tier 3 Smithing and Tailoring at profession Level 22, with clearer crafting feedback.
6. **Dungeon Polish:** See steadier combat status rows for enemies, shields and effects, with more readable dungeon encounters.
7. **Sounds of Veldryn:** Hear original music and combat effects, plus special reveal sounds for Epic, Legendary and Mythic equipment.
8. **Everyday Improvements:** Enjoy more polished Guild, Skills and Inventory screens, expanded translations and dated What's New entries.

**Date:** Leave blank in the app release-note record until real publication. The What's New UI renders the saved `published_at` when a release is published.
**Size:** Eight highlights; each under the mobile release-note parser's 200-character limit.

## Potential 1.0.11 headline upgrades — explicitly NOT in the eight draft highlights

The following work could make the release larger, but **must not be advertised or assumed enabled** until its own release gates pass:

1. **Equipment rolls (T1–T5+):** #1404 is an unreleased, integration-only 7-stat rollout candidate. Requires final exact-head CI, save/receipt parity, all combat-mode effects, Android QA and production deployment sign-off.
2. **Premium gathering tools:** #1435 proposes cumulative Tier 1–4 reductions (10/15/15/20), staggered blueprint hunts and rarer drops. Draft, not approved/merged to main as a release.
3. **Dungeon Endurance + dungeon-only weekly quests + friend/guild Echo selection:** #1438 is a draft and needs staging migrations, deterministic rewards, queue compatibility, account privacy and Android QA.
4. **World Bosses and Party Events:** hidden integration and economy/loot work is ongoing; neither raid/chest system has a verified public launch path. Keep out until release gate approval.
5. **Player Market, Guild PvE, public Rankings, Classes/Skills/Enemies/Equipment 2.0:** separate hidden/WIP tracks, not launch promises for 1.0.11.
