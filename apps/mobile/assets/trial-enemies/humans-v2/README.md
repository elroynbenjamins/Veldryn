# Companion trial enemy art

These 29 runtime assets use the approved Veldryn enemy direction established on 2026-10-30.

## Shared rules

- Every trial enemy is visibly human, including enemies whose names reference animals, wyrms, shades, or other creatures.
- Artwork is a complete head-to-boots character cutout on a transparent background, not a rectangular portrait.
- Rendering follows the existing Veldryn companion language: hand-painted fantasy detail, crisp controlled edges, a strong square-readable silhouette, and faction motifs carried through equipment and magic.
- Runtime files are 256 x 256 PNGs with alpha, derived from built-in image-generation originals.

## Visual hierarchy

### Regular enemies

The first 18 formation enemies are muted, worn, and deliberately less collectible-looking. They use aged metal, dusty cloth, weathered leather, one subdued faction accent, and compact effects.

### Checkpoint enemies

The six checkpoint enemies have stronger silhouettes, clearer faction colors, and one signature effect. They remain below the headline bosses in ornament and effect volume.

### Headline bosses

The five `BOSS COMPANION` enemies receive the most distinctive silhouettes, ornate equipment, stronger faction colors, and dramatic signature magic. Their palettes remain coherent rather than multicolored.

## Generation baseline

Built-in image generation was used with approved Veldryn companion artwork as direct style and faction reference. The common production prompt required:

> A complete human character from head to boots, dynamic three-quarter combat pose, centered on a genuinely transparent square canvas; polished hand-painted Veldryn fantasy game art, crisp edges, readable silhouette, no animal anatomy, no portrait backdrop, no scenery, no frame, no text, and no watermark.

Each character then received a unique role, weapon, body type, age or gender direction, faction palette, and tier-specific effect budget.
