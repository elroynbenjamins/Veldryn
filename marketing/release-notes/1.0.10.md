# VELDRYN 1.0.10 — Stronger Bonds

**Status:** Unpublished release-copy concept updated 15 November 2026. The Android client has been version-bumped to 1.0.10 (versionCode 59) in source, but this document does **not** publish an in-game announcement, upload an AAB, deploy the backend, change the minimum required version, or enable hidden content.

**Basis:** Candidate player-facing changes recorded in GitHub main on 14–15 November 2026 after the prepared 1.0.9 release notes. Do not repeat 1.0.9 features as new. Every outward-facing claim must be checked against the exact signed Android 1.0.10 bundle, production migrations, gameplay/coop Edge functions, and verified rollout. Main-branch code alone does not prove a feature is already live.

## Release concept

**Theme:** Build stronger bonds with your Companions, return safely to the heart of a Dungeon run, and feel the benefit of more rewarding crafting and smoother everyday play.

**Player promise:** Companion growth is easier to follow, Dungeon preparation and recovery are more dependable, crafting feels more rewarding, and combat, Guild, chat, inventory and profile screens take fewer taps to use.

## In-game What's New — six highlights

**Title:** Stronger Bonds

1. **Stronger Companion Bonds:** Follow Bond XP and upcoming rewards at a glance, claim ready milestones more directly, and manage Quarters without digging through menus.
2. **Smoother Dungeon Runs:** Resume interrupted boss rooms, choose your Dungeon tonic and optional auto-use, view Expedition Marks before claiming, and stay connected with Live party chat.
3. **More Rewarding Crafting:** Earn an additional 20% equipment-crafting XP at Tiers 2–4, spend two fewer primary materials per equipment recipe, and recover more materials through Salvage.
4. **Better Social Play:** Read chat messages and emotes more easily, keep Party Chat visible above the keyboard, and find friends or renew expired party recruitment more reliably.
5. **Clearer Combat & Activities:** See active combat on Home, read class-skill bonuses that match actual effects, and enjoy cleaner activity controls and crafting screens.
6. **Inventory, Guild & Profile Polish:** Preview selling and Salvage outcomes, handle bulk equipment more safely, find Guild Project rewards faster, and inspect player badges more easily.

**Copy consideration:** Bond rank progression is intentionally about three times longer than before. This must be explained in the detailed notes and validated for existing saved progress; do not imply Bond levelling is faster.

## Google Play — What's New

Grow closer to your Companions with clearer Bond progress, rewards and Quarters. Enjoy smoother Dungeons with boss-room recovery, tonic selection, Expedition Mark previews and Live party chat. Earn more XP from Tier 2–4 equipment crafting, use fewer key materials and get better Salvage returns. Plus clearer combat activities, friend search, more readable chat, safer inventory actions, Guild alerts and profile polish.

## Detailed Release Notes

### Companion Bonds and Sanctuary

- Reworked Bond progression panels to show current Bond XP, rank requirements, next milestones and claimable rewards without hiding the most useful information.
- Bond rank progression uses an approximately **3× longer curve** than the previous balance, with versioned progress preservation for existing companions. Verify migration and reward entitlement outcomes before rollout.
- Owned Companions are easier to locate and switch. Claimable Bond milestone rewards can be opened directly from the roster or Companion Index.
- Companion Quarters are surfaced directly within Sanctuary, with a clearer companion selector and tier information rather than being buried behind a disclosure.
- Bond progress is also visible from Training, with tighter layouts, localized descriptions and fewer overlapping entry points.
- Active Companion Trial members can assist in eligible Dungeons while respecting the existing availability and role rules.

### Dungeon preparation, combat and recovery

- Interrupted Dungeon runs can revalidate and reconcile pending combat actions on re-entry, including the previously troublesome boss-room transition. Retry handling is scoped to the same run to avoid duplicate actions.
- Dungeon preparation includes a compact tonic chooser, saved per-character preference and optional automatic preparation at the server-authorized first-room window. Live queue setup reflects the selected tonic.
- The Dungeon reward presentation shows the Expedition Marks award before the final reward claim.
- Live Dungeon runs add a persistent party-chat dock and expandable composer, with preserved drafts on send errors and no duplicate sends.
- Echo Dungeon combat settlement and its preview presentation have been refined.
- The Dungeon activity bar and redundant navigation controls are reduced during active runs, keeping attention on the encounter.
- Do not claim new tonic consumption limits, new Dungeon tiers, or guaranteed new rewards without verifying the corresponding published gameplay rules.

### Crafting, materials and equipment economy

- Final assembly of **T2, T3 and T4 equipment grants a further 20% XP** on top of the existing shared 25% T2–T4 crafting XP uplift. This applies to equipment recipes, not every profession action.
- Primary equipment material requirements are reduced by **two units per recipe**, without removing special tier binders or overriding other material requirements.
- Equipment Salvage recovery increases by **five percentage points**. Preview the actual recovered resources before confirming; protected and equipped items retain their safeguards.
- The T2 Riverheart Draught recipe no longer requires Dewleaf and instead uses **25 River Mint**. Existing in-progress brewing reservations receive compatibility protection.
- The Forge and Equipment Pouch have clearer batch collection, selected-item counts, protected-item counts, estimated Gold sales and complete Salvage previews.
- Sell/Salvage confirmations provide more explicit settlement outcomes; do not assert that equipment resale percentages increased without a separate verified balance change.
- Equipment costs and displayed stats receive consistent number rounding, while crafting and gathering actions use tighter controls and availability explanations.

### Combat, skills, home and activities

- The Home current-activity panel can display active hunting animations alongside gathering activity presentation.
- Passive class-skill tooltips are derived more closely from real runtime stat effects across all nine classes, reducing mismatches between descriptions and applied bonuses.
- Knife Dancer Precision receives accuracy/evasion reward corrections and corresponding display/test coverage.
- The extra Queue Activity action is removed from the Skills overview, reducing duplicate navigation.
- Gathering availability and Forge Claim All summaries are easier to scan.
- Character slot progression for slots 3–5 receives slightly higher unlock requirements; verify that legacy unlocks remain preserved before publishing precise thresholds.

### Friends, Party, Guild and chat

- Party Chat keeps recent messages in view as the Android keyboard opens, matching the behavior of public and Guild channels.
- Inline emotes and ordinary chat messages use more readable sizes and spacing.
- Player-directory name search fixes SQL wildcard escaping and retains safe prefix matching and relationship privacy rules. This requires the matching production migration.
- Friendship and party recruitment messaging are clearer, with an option to renew expired recruitment.
- Active Live Dungeon chat is easier to reach without leaving the run.
- Claim-ready Guild Projects can surface an Account alert linking to the relevant Projects view.

### Profiles, badges, inventory and reliability

- Tapping a player badge shows more useful badge information.
- VIP and VIP+ profile badges can be selected where the corresponding entitlement exists; badge state remains distinct from achievements and other featured items.
- Equipment Pouch and Inventory bulk sell/Salvage now preview selected, eligible and protected counts and explain final payouts more clearly.
- Character deletion protects gear recovery and fixes Android text-entry issues during confirmation.
- Various interrupted action and stale-game-version transitions are handled more safely during Dungeon resume and background gameplay.
- Localized interface labels and compact mobile layouts have received additional refinement.

## Not 1.0.10 launch claims

- **World Bosses:** the 5-day preparation / 2-day weekend battle concept, three attempts, multi-character participation, War Camp and combat calibration remain unreleased until the server-side event, settlement and availability rules are production validated.
- **Guild PvE:** Broken Forge content, source-engine calibration, V7 previews and the V8 shared-run emulator are test-only/design work, not a live Guild mode.
- **Party Events:** provisional boss balancing, loadout tests and event systems are not publicly playable merely because prototypes exist.
- **Public Leaderboards and Arena:** do not advertise rankings or PvP as released until their visibility and server-authoritative seasons are enabled.
- **Player Market, Dungeon Marks Shop and Ashlands:** remain gated, unfinished or In Development.
- **Quest Milestones:** mention only when the account/character progression, eligibility, claim flow and production rollout have all been verified; design discussions are not proof of a live system.
