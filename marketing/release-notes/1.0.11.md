# VELDRYN 1.0.11 — Beyond the Depths

**Status:** Unpublished 1.0.11 release candidate · 20 November 2026 · full exact-head CI, staging/backend parity, signed Android AAB and Google Play rollout still require release approval.
**Baseline:** Veldryn 1.0.10 is listed on Google Play (indexed update date 17 November 2026); public app rollout is separate from GitHub source merges.
**Theme:** Deeper and more varied Dungeons, stronger equipment and gathering progression, familiar Echo allies, and polished everyday play.
**Scope rule:** The eight highlights below are **candidates**, not statements of shipped 1.0.11 features. Confirm exact release binary + production backend availability before promoting any of them.

## In-game What's New — eight candidate highlights

**Title:** Beyond the Depths

1. **Equipment Rolls:** Forge Tier 1+ gear with bonus stat rolls; previously owned equipment stays intact.
2. **Gathering Tools:** Stack tool tiers for stronger Mining, Woodcutting and Fishing speed bonuses.
3. **Dungeon Endurance:** Brave tougher rooms and choose boss encounters at seven-room checkpoints.
4. **Dungeon Weeklies:** Complete dedicated challenges for Expedition Marks, separate from regular Quests.
5. **Familiar Allies:** Recruit eligible friends and guildmates as role-balanced Solo Dungeon Echoes.
6. **Dungeon Discoveries:** Explore more boons and artifacts, with clearer room choices and combat previews.
7. **Better Daily Progress:** Enjoy Supply Journey boosts, Companion XP banking and clearer Cooking XP.
8. **Realm Polish:** A cleaner character roster and Rewards Inbox, improved Guild cues, audio controls, chat and transitions.

**Publication date:** Only set when the verified 1.0.11 rollout actually happens. Do not publish draft entries.
**Source status:** The five headline workstreams and later Dungeon, Character, Inbox, Guild and audio refinements are consolidated in the guarded 1.0.11 source candidate (including #1479 and #1480). None of this confirms a public 1.0.11 rollout.
