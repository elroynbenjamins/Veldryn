# VELDRYN 1.0.12 — Smoother Adventures

**Status:** Owner-approved editorial concept · 24 November 2026 · **unpublished**. Approval of wording is not approval of rollout or final feature readiness.
**Title:** Smoother Adventures
**Target:** Android 1.0.12.

## In-game What's New — nine highlights

1. **Dungeons:** Improved Q-Mode level scaling, stronger Echo equipment relevance, smoother room progression and clearer rewards.
2. **Gathering Tools:** Dedicated tier management, active and reserve tool slots, Skill XP bonus rolls and improved tool transfers.
3. **Equipment:** Refined item inspection, clearer stat comparisons and the ability to inspect equipment on other players' profiles.
4. **Cooking:** New Combat XP meal, combined Meals tab, stackable buff durations up to 24 hours and rebalanced recipes.
5. **Social:** Larger chat emotes and text, unique Party names, improved Social Name consistency and active character profiles.
6. **Economy:** Rebalanced Cooking and crafting recipes, resource requirements, Gold costs and skill XP rewards.
7. **Quality of Life:** Seamless regional music, refined shortcuts, improved profile cosmetics and cleaner interfaces.
8. **Performance:** Optimized server requests, improved crafting responsiveness, dungeon synchronization, stability and various bug fixes.

9. **Frostfall:** The winter festival draws near, with seasonal adventures and festive rewards awaiting.
