# Equipment 2.0 — T4 replacement set catalogue review v1

**23 November 2026 · HIDDEN REVIEW ONLY · no runtime activation or main merge**

The former Citadel and Bloodsteel T4 drafts were promoted into rare T5 sets.
These two ordinary Glassbound families refill their exact T4 catalogue and
slot footprints, preserving the planned 48-set / 204-member structure.

## Glassbound Bastionmail — six-piece standard tank set

Replaces the retired T4 Citadel footprint: helmet, chest, legs, boots, cape,
and ring. Its identity is dependable mitigation and recovery without the
rare T5 family's authored innate percentage stats.

- **2 pieces — Steady Links:** +3% Defense.
- **4 pieces — Braced Formation:** after blocking or mitigating an eligible
  direct hit, gain +4% Defense for 5 seconds; 14-second internal cooldown.
- **6 pieces — Hold the Line:** crossing below 35% HP grants a shield equal
  to 5% maximum HP for 6 seconds; 35-second internal cooldown.

## Glassbound Ashguard — four-piece standard tank set

Replaces the retired T4 Bloodsteel footprint: gloves, boots, amulet, and ring.
It is the slightly more aggressive ordinary T4 tank option, but does not use
fixed innate percentage stats or rare-set crafting premiums.

- **2 pieces — Ashen Grip:** +2% Defense and +2% Attack.
- **4 pieces — Guarded Return:** after eligible direct HP damage, the next
  basic attack within 5 seconds deals 10% increased damage; 10-second
  internal cooldown. Damage-over-time, reflection, self-damage, and proc
  loops cannot trigger it.

Both families inherit the approved standard T4 rarity, quality, affix,
wear-level, crafting, provenance, and Player Market rules. The companion JSON
defines exact replacement and member identities; it intentionally does not
invent new material quantities outside the authoritative T4 recipe table.
