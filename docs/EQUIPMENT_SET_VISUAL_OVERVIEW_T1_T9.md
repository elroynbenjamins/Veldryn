# T1-T9 equipment and skin overview

This is the review index for the 243 live sets in
`../apps/mobile/src/content/equipment_catalog_t1_t9_v33.json`: nine classes,
three paths per class, nine tiers, ten pieces per set. Names and IDs below
come from that catalog. The short phrases are proposed visual motifs, not
new gameplay items or recipe ingredients.

Read each cell with `EQUIPMENT_SKIN_ART_DIRECTION.md`: retain the class's
current color and weapon/offhand identity; use the class-path silhouette,
then apply the tier's construction quality and level-appropriate regional
resources. Foundation, Specialist, and Alternate must look different in
grayscale at inventory size. Adjacent tiers of one path should look like
improvements to the same design, not a recolor or unrelated costume.

For skins, use the male and female in-game mannequins from
`../apps/mobile/assets/character-base-v1/` and the accepted pilot in
`../apps/mobile/art-review/source-pack/VELDRYN_Character_Runtime_Assets_v1/source_references/ironwarden_pilot_reference.png`.
Preserve face, hair, anatomy, pose, ground line, pixel scale, and backdrop.
Show the listed set's helmet, chest, cape, weapon, and offhand consistently
with its ten-piece equipment atlas. These are front-view production skins;
no new back view is required for T1-T9.
