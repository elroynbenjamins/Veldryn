## Wayfinder T1 review candidates

The next-class first-tier candidates are stored under `T1_010-falcon-mark`,
`T1_011-greenstride-skirmisher`, and `T1_012-startracker`. Each folder contains
one male and one female front portrait aligned to the immutable Veldryn pilot
mannequin. The runtime-bound `male-front.png` and `female-front.png` files are
the revised v2 portraits; the earlier `*-front-v1.png` candidates remain for
comparison. Runtime copies are bound in `../../assets/character-skins-t1-wayfinder/`.

## Bastion T1 accepted front-view skins

These six transparent RGBA v3 portraits use the same male and female
mannequins, 1024 x 1536 canvas, and front framing. Female hair is clear of the
helmet crown. Each hammer follows the Ironwarden weapon-down stance: the hand
grips near the upper end, a short butt shows above it, and the hammer head
hangs below the hand beside the leg. Their equipment follows the matching
normalized icon sheets. All three male/female v3 pairs are accepted. Earlier
portraits remain beside v3 for comparison. Runtime copies are bound in
`../../assets/character-skins-t1-bastion/`.

## Dreadguard T1 accepted front-view skins

These six v1 portraits use the fixed male and female mannequins on matching
1024 x 1536 transparent RGBA canvases. Their helmets, burgundy cloth, shields,
and chained weapon heads follow the corresponding Dreadguard equipment icon

near the upper end; female hair remains under the helmets. Doom Bastion's male
v2 refines the cap, mask, and collar fit; v1 remains for comparison. All three

## Ravager T1 review candidates

These six front portraits are the beginner-tier Ravager pass, aligned to the
immutable male and female mannequins on 1024 x 1536 canvases. Each skin now
matches its atlas-specific helmet and weapon silhouette, with the set's small
tooth, howl, or wedge motifs repeated across the armor. The outfits stay
novice-scale: patched cloth, worn leather, rough weapon wraps, and small
mismatched plates rather than polished or heavily ornamented armor. The
war charm remains distinct from the necklace/amulet, especially on Sundering.
Runtime copies are bound in
`../../assets/character-skins-t1-ravager/`.
