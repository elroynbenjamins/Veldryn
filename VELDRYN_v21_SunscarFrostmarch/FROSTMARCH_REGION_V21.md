# VELDRYN v21 — Frostmarch Region III Foundation

Frostmarch is promoted from roadmap data into implementation-ready versioned content.

## Progression
- Region: `REG_003`
- Approximate level span: 45–70
- Entry: defeat the Sand Tyrant / finish Sunscar departure story
- Capstone: `BOSS_003 Frost Wyrm`
- Completion unlock: Ashlands + next progression cap

## Zones
1. **Thawgate** — Lv45–50. Fortified northern gate; Frostiron, raiders and the last dependable shelter.
2. **Whitepine Reach** — Lv48–56. Snow forest; false trails, treants, predators, Whitepine and Rime Resin.
3. **Shiverlake** — Lv52–60. Frozen lake and under-ice ruins; Icefin, Bellfin and trapped memories.
4. **Choir Caverns** — Lv57–67. Singing ice caves; ordered interrupts, Rimeglass, Choir Bloom and relic fragments.
5. **Wyrmspine** — Lv62–70. High mountain lair; breath lines, Ice Prison mechanics and the Frost Wyrm.

## Combat roster
The existing 16-monster Frostmarch roster is preserved and upgraded to the v20 combat-stat contract: Max HP, Power, Accuracy, Armor, Ward, Evasion, Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Haste, Tenacity, Healing Power and Shielding Power.

Telegraphed pass/fail mechanics remain deterministic by default; major mechanics do not randomly crit.

## Bosses
- **Needlefang Matriarch** — Whitepine Hunt dungeon boss.
- **The Bellbeast Below** — Shiverlake Descent dungeon boss; preserves the older Bellbeast concept.
- **The Hollow Cantor** — Choir Caverns dungeon boss; preserves the older Cantor concept.
- **Frost Wyrm** — regional capstone.

## Co-op
All three support both existing modes:
- Live: strict 1 Tank / 2 Damage / 1 Support.
- Q-Mode: separate asynchronous alternative.

Content:
- `COP_007 Whitepine Hunt` — Lv52+, 4–6 nodes, true-beast tracking through false trails.
- `COP_008 Shiverlake Descent` — Lv58+, 5–7 nodes, under-ice route/oxygen pressure and Bellbeast.
- `COP_009 Choir Caverns` — Lv64+, 6–7 nodes, ordered interrupt sequences and Hollow Cantor.

## Regional resources
The existing ten-resource set is preserved: Whitepine Log, Rime Resin, Frostiron, Rimeglass, Wintermint, Choir Bloom, Icefin, Bellfin Scale, Wyrm Scale and Frozen Heart.

## Story
The existing ten-beat main story is preserved from Thawgate through Black Snow and the Ashlands transition.

## Gameplay depth
v21 also defines:
- 8 regional side quests
- 5 repeatable activities
- 6 achievements
- Frostmarch Monsters collection book
- 4 Echo conditions
- 4 relic-mechanic hooks
- 5 Pets + 4 Companion unlock-source hooks
- 3 weather rules: Light Snow, Blizzard, Aurora
- 3 regional Contract templates
- 4 Frost Wyrm mastery tiers

## Equipment is deliberately excluded
No Frostmarch weapons, armor or set definitions from the old spreadsheet are authoritative in v21. They remain `draft_do_not_implement` until the expanded-stat equipment-budget pass. Dungeon and boss rewards use materials, cosmetics, relic hooks and `generic_equipment_reward_hook` only.
