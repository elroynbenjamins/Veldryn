## Implementation
Extend the private exact-input QA adapter with three source-defined incoming damage reductions: Heliarch Guard (6% actual max-HP loss threshold, 5% next-hit reduction, 6s window, 20s cooldown); Unyielding Frame (6%, 4%, 6s, 18s); Rimevanguard (7%, 6% timed reduction, 5s, 22s). One source record per mechanic. Restrict these IDs to single QA Ironwarden actor; offensive IDs remain Ravager-only. No mixing authority or live ownership. Scope is test-only, no new mint or set custody.

Engine hook after ordinary core modifiers, before shield/HP resolution; post-hit earning from actual pre/post HP difference. No fatal hit earning, true damage, generated secondary IDs or unrecognized enemy skills/phase IDs. Next-hit charges require a direct hit whose unreduced damage would penetrate shields; timed Rimevanguard covers eligible original enemy direct or periodic damage. Same timestamp cannot benefit from newly earned reduction. Strongest active reduction only, cap 15%, weaker one-use charge retained until consumed/expired; cooldown starts on earning. These conservative scope, same-tick and strongest-only rules are QA design choices, not final approved stacking. No modification of normal no-token path. Existing legacy Edge shim denies this private authority; generated bundles not rebuilt/deployed.
