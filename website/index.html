Before you begin

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to start?

You can start as a guest. Secure your account later using the in-game account settings to help protect your progress.

Can I play at my own pace?

Yes. Veldryn is designed around idle activities and offline progression, subject to your in-game offline reserve.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Veldryn includes friends, guilds, parties and cooperative dungeons. Some features unlock as you progress.

Where can I get support or report a bug?

Join the official Veldryn Discord for community help and feedback. For privacy and account-related requests, see the privacy policy.
