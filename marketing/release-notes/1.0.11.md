# VELDRYN 1.0.11 — Beyond the Depths

**Status:** Final-copy candidate · updated 21 November 2026 · **not published**. Source integration [#1511](https://github.com/elroynbenjamins/Veldryn/pull/1511) is merged into `main` (followed by additional fixes); this does **not** mean the player-facing release, production services, or store build are live.
**Release note title:** Beyond the Depths
**Target:** Android 1.0.11. Final signed AAB, Google Play rollout and production backend readiness must be verified separately.

## In-game What's New — eight highlights

1. **Equipment Rolls:** New T1–T5 gear can roll useful class-neutral bonus stats, while existing equipment keeps its progress.
2. **Gathering Tools:** Stack tool tiers and forge individual rarity, quality and one Speed or Rare Find bonus per tool.
3. **Dungeon Endurance:** Explore tougher rooms and choose a boss encounter at every seven-room checkpoint.
4. **Dungeon Weeklies:** Take on dedicated weekly Dungeon challenges for Expedition Marks.
5. **Friends & Guild Echoes:** Recruit eligible friends or guildmates as Solo Dungeon allies.
6. **Deeper Dungeons:** Discover more boons and artifacts, plus quicker enemy previews and clearer room choices.
7. **Everyday Progress:** Enjoy Supply Journey boosts, Companion XP banking, Cooking XP previews and extra Class Challenges.
8. **More Polish:** Clearer Skills and recipes, Gear Storage details, improved Character Roster, Inbox, Guild contributions and audio controls.

**Important:** These describe the planned 1.0.11 release candidate, not already-live production features. Publish only once the exact build and production services are approved.

## Feature detail and eligibility

- **Crafted gear:** The underlying weapon and non-weapon roll engine supports T1–T9, but only T1–T5 gear is currently player-accessible and therefore advertised. Available rolls use class-neutral stats with tier/rarity scaling. Legacy equipment and old receipt versions remain valid; do not imply every legacy copy has gained a roll or that the full Equipment 2.0 system is released. Final combat/Forge/service validation still required.
- **Gathering:** The clean candidate #1511 includes approved #1507 and #1510: four stacking tool tiers, per-copy rarity, quality 0–100, exactly one Speed (+2–8%) or Rare Find (+2–5%) stat, effective 80% action-time reduction ceiling, and localized base-vs-roll breakdown. Legacy copies remain unchanged. Only eligible gathering rare drops benefit; at present Mining has such drops, while Woodcutting and Fishing have no corresponding rare-drop tables. Final server, staging and device QA remain required.
- **Dungeons:** Endurance provides escalating encounters and a boss choice after each seven-room checkpoint. Dedicated Dungeon weeklies award Expedition Marks, and eligible friend/Guild Echoes can fill Solo parties under role/eligibility checks. Four supporting Dungeon migrations have been checked on **staging**, not production; all claims depend on production deployment and smoke tests.
- **Core improvements:** Supply Journey/Boost Vault, Companion XP banking, Cooking XP preview, Class Challenges, compact Skills Navigator, better tool Recipe routing, Guild contribution/donation guidance, compact Rewards Inbox, Gear Storage item inspection and Character Roster with the third slot at 800 account skill (earned legacy slots preserved).
- **Sound:** Per-region/activity music controls and approved original Town/Asterfall tracks are in the mainline candidate. Do not advertise the remaining five unapproved replacement themes. Improved transitions respect reduced-motion settings.

## Exclusions — not public 1.0.11 promises

World Bosses, Party Events, Player Market, Guild PvE, public Rankings and full Combat/Equipment/Classes/Skills 2.0 stay hidden. The rolled gathering-tool affixes from #1507/#1510 are integrated into #1511 but remain subject to final release QA; separate, unapproved weapon-roll UI drafts are not release promises. Do not advertise locked equipment tiers T6–T9.
