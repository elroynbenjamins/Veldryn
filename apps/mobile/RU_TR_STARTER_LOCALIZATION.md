# Russian and Turkish starter localization

Russian (`ru`) and Turkish (`tr`) are selectable alongside English, German, Spanish, Dutch, Italian and French.

The starter dictionary currently covers more than 450 common interface phrases per language, including character creation, navigation, Settings, save recovery, dungeon rooms/voting, Echo, reward states, Party recruitment and private invitations. Missing phrases deliberately fall back to English; these are not yet complete game localizations.

This 1.0.6 redesign pass also localizes the current Party and Recruitment controls in the five established translations and adds all eight languages to the Party communication-preference selector. The Party database allowlist must be deployed before selecting the newly supported options in a production backend.

**Still open:** native-speaker copy review, narrow-phone text expansion checks, broader gameplay/item/quest translations, and a fresh audit of older catalog keys after the screen redesigns settle. Do not treat the older six-language social coverage snapshot as a current completeness report.
