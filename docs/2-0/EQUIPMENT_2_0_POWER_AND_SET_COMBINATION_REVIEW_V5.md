## Pass A — Equipment power budget

Source-pinned 169 original Forge templates plus 64 T5 standard/armament candidates (233 rows). All 9 classes have source-resolved complete T1–T5 role-matching gear references (45 loadouts), with 20 ten-slot one-handed and 25 nine-slot two/dual-handed cases. T5 armament profiles inherit the matching T4 source as explicit proposals. No T5 source becomes registered. All 45 are template fixtures, not owned items.

Use the source base budget (slot budget × tier × 1.08 × rarity × quality), preserving Attack + Defense + HP/4. A two/dual-handed weapon receives the weapon plus offhand budget; a one-handed weapon receives only its own. The quality and rarity multiplier is applied once, and does not multiply affixes.

Provisional **equipment index** = weighted base points × (1 + sum of effective random-affix fractions × proposed channel weights). The weights are review assumptions, not calibrated combat utility: defense 1, HP 1, ward 1.2, tenacity 0.5, crit chance 1.5, crit damage 0.5, haste 1.4, penetration 1, accuracy 0.8, ability damage 1, healing power 1. The model retains raw/effective values separately and applies V4 caps once per channel. Quality changes base only. Innate effects, gems, set mechanics, class level, Skills and companions are **unpriced**, not worthless or approved as zero. No matchmaking/rank/Market usage. Real class contribution and Combat 2.0 utility weights remain open D3.

3,240 deterministic source-template cases: 9 classes × 5 tiers × 6 rarities × 3 qualities (0/50/100) × 4 affix selections (distributed, crit-damage focus, defense focus, healing focus). These are not a statistical random sample and not combat encounters.

Independent calculation on the committed input fixtures: 360 comparison groups; 24 exceed a provisional 5% hand-group gap tolerance; worst gap about 6.57%. T5 Mythic Q50: distributed +6.57%, crit damage +4.63%, defense +5.36%, healing +4.95% in the one-handed group's favor. Source base-budget spread is only 0.025 points at that configuration from rounding. Ten versus nine physical items yield 30 versus 27 Mythic random rolls at T3–T5. This is a balance-pressure finding, not proof of class damage imbalance. Next compare compensation options: (A) an extra unique hand-budget roll allowance on two/dual-handed weapons, versus (B) stronger effective weapon roll bands under an equal total hand budget. Do not approve either until rarity/stacking/custody/economy and engine benchmarks are compared. No phantom offhand, no per-class random pool, no hidden stat inflation.
