# VELDRYN 1.0.13 — Frostfall Festival

**Status:** Android release reported live by owner · in-game notes published 27 November 2026 at 20:37:34 UTC.
**Title:** Frostfall Festival
**Target:** Android 1.0.13.

## In-game What's New — nine highlights

1. **Frostfall:** Begins December 3! Earn seasonal reputation, Frostbell Tokens, festive pets, cosmetics, emotes and three new Elite Companions.
2. **Aurora Hollow:** Venture into the seasonal dungeon across five difficulty tiers and face the Frostfall Guild Boss.
3. **Winter Hearth:** Unite your guild to donate logs, craft Christmas Lights, unlock shared bonuses and earn permanent festive guild cosmetics.
4. **Faith & Pets:** Equip one blessing per category and quickly manage blessings and your active pet from Current Activity. Faith training XP has been rebalanced.
5. **Companions:** Earn 100, 150 or 200 daily Sanctuary XP per eligible Companion, depending on Training Ground tier. All Companion unlocks are class-neutral.
6. **Gathering & Stats:** Seasonal and weather gathering bonuses now improve speed; Herbalism modifiers and character and equipment stat explanations are clearer.
7. **Dungeons & Rewards:** Fixed Endurance weekly quest tracking, Dungeon Shop collectible ownership and reward claim issues.
8. **Profiles & Soundtrack:** Improved animated cosmetic displays and public showcases, with expanded regional and encounter music.
9. **Performance & Quality of Life:** Faster loading, smoother character switching and offline recovery, fewer unnecessary requests and improved small-screen layouts.

Frostfall content becomes available during the scheduled event, subject to verified server activation; this is not an announcement that the event is already live.
