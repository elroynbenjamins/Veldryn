# VELDRYN 1.0.11 — Everyday Improvements

**Release candidate:** Stable mainline subset. Not published; Android signing, Play versionCode, final QA and owner go/no-go are separate.
**Scope:** Changes verified as incorporated into `main` by 20 November 2026. Does not include the unmerged Dungeon Endurance, new class-neutral all-tier stat-roll system, cumulative tool investment, uncleared sample-based soundtrack tracks, World Bosses, Party Events, Player Market or Equipment/Combat/Classes 2.0.

## Player-facing changes

- **Skills:** compact one-row skill navigator and improved filters. Mining/Woodcutting/Fishing tool Recipe links open the precise Smithing tool entry and retain Back navigation.
- **Inventory & rewards:** compact Rewards Inbox and shared Gear Storage equipment detail inspection, with more useful bonus-stat information.
- **Characters:** compact Character Roster, with the third character slot unlocked at a total Account Skill level of 800 and previously unlocked slots preserved.
- **Music:** one scrollable Music & Audio page with individual region/activity settings; original synthesized Town (*Lanterns at Hearth*) and Asterfall (*First Light*) themes. The remaining five replacement tracks are not cleared for release.
- **Polish:** subtle screen transitions, improved Guild donation and daily contribution guidance, Bondstone artwork on the Fallen Knight victory reward, and optimized CI workflows.
- **Other mainline work:** already integrated daily supplies, Companion XP banking, Cooking improvements, class challenges and crafting progression; verify actual production services and visual QA before featuring them as release headlines.

## Google Play English draft

Explore a cleaner Skills hub and Rewards Inbox, a clearer Character Roster (third slot at 800 Account Skill), and full Gear Storage item details. Tool Recipe links now open the right Smithing recipe. Customize music by region and activity, enjoy original Town and Asterfall music, and discover smoother screen transitions, Guild donation guidance, and reward presentation.
