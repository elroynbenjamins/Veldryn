# VELDRYN 1.0.9 — Forge the Edge

**Status:** Unpublished release-copy concept prepared 12 November 2026. This file does **not** publish the update, change the required client version, deploy backend services, or enable release-gated systems.

**Basis:** Player-facing work completed or staged for the 1.0.9 candidate after the published 1.0.8 announcement. Features from 1.0.8 are not re-announced. Final copy must be reduced to features verified in the production Android build and its matching backend deployment.

## Release concept

**Theme:** Your equipment build has more bite. Tier 5 gear now costs what it should and introduces the game's first secondary combat affixes, hunts feel more physical with strike presentation and pixel-art scenes, and profiles become more expressive.

**Player promise:** Equipment should reward the time it takes to craft. T5 pieces now sit between T4 and T6 in cost and materials, three slots carry secondary stats that scale with crafted quality, and the world around you responds with richer visual feedback.

## In-game What's New

**Title:** Forge the Edge

1. **T5 Economy Rebalanced:** Tier 5 crafting costs now scale smoothly between T4 and T6. Gold, materials and acquisition time all increase monotonically — no more T5 being cheaper than T4.
2. **T5 Forged Affixes:** Gloves gain Haste, Amulets gain Crit Rate, and Boots gain Evasion. Affix strength scales from 2% to 5% based on crafted rarity and quality, with harder recipes and extra profession XP.
3. **Pixel Gathering Scenes:** Mining, woodcutting and fishing show animated regional backgrounds with material-accurate ore, timber and gathering character sprites.
4. **Sharper Combat Presentation:** New hunt strike sprites, impact bursts and crit effects make hit-by-hit combat more readable and more responsive.
5. **Profile & Badge Polish:** Expanded badge showcase, profile wardrobe improvements, player styled names and a refined public profile scene.
6. **Dungeon Visual Polish:** Vote countdowns, route meters, boss telegraphs, loadout stat deltas and victory moments all received animation and readability improvements.

## Detailed Release Notes

### Equipment and economy

- Tier 5 base Gold repriced from 3,500 to 6,000, producing monotonic full-set costs: T4 (~136k Smithing / ~118k Tailoring) < T5 (~195k) < T6 (~340k).
- T5 material quantities rebalanced so that Smithing and Tailoring both require more regional materials than T4, using the 1.25× tier material multiplier.
- Every T5 piece requires one Sunfused Fixative (two for affix slots), preserving the Scorpion Venom bottleneck as a meaningful tier gate.
- T5 farm-time bands widened to 0.75–6.2 hours per piece to accommodate the affix material multiplier while remaining below T6 floors.

### T5 forged affixes

- Three of ten T5 equipment slots carry a secondary combat stat: Gloves (Haste), Amulet (Crit Rate), Boots (Evasion).
- Affix percentage scales with crafted rarity and quality: Common/low-quality copies receive 2%, Epic baseline 3%, strong Rare/Epic copies 4%, and exceptional Legendary/Mythic results reach 5%.
- Affix slots cost 15% more regional materials, 10% more crafting time, one extra Sunfused Fixative, and award 20% more profession XP.
- Affix stats flow through the full combat pipeline: equipment build stats, combat modifiers (speed, incoming damage, expected crit) and effective stats all reflect equipped T5 affixes.
- Item inspection displays the affix stat, percentage, rarity and quality on equipped T5 pieces.

### Combat and hunt presentation

- Hunt strike presentation upgraded with new attack sprites, impact burst effects and critical-hit visuals that adapt to class and enemy.
- Hunting preview expanded with richer stat previews and preparation information.
- Combat presentation logic refined for clearer wounded/finishing state transitions and narration.

### Gathering and pixel scenes

- New pixel-art gathering scene system with animated regional backgrounds for caves, forests and waterways across Asterfall, Sunscar, Frostmarch and Ashlands.
- Material-accurate ore chunks, timber logs and gathering character sprites (mining, chopping, fishing cast/wait/pull) at 1×/2×/3× density.
- Sprite animation framework with frame-based playback, motion easing and composable sprite layers.
- Gathering scene preview added to the development browser review tool.

### Profiles and identity

- Profile badge showcase expanded with new settings UI, gallery and ownership information.
- Profile wardrobe chips redesigned for clearer cosmetic selection.
- Player styled names refined with improved rendering and fallback behavior.
- Public profile scene and profile stage polish: updated layout, scene preview and showcase panel.
- Profile badge showcase v3 migration prepared.

### Dungeon visual polish

- Live vote countdown shows urgent tint and pulse animation as time runs out.
- Route decision cards display a proportional vote meter under each choice.
- Boss telegraph events show a phase-threshold bar with a pulsing dot indicator.
- Dungeon shop offers display category glyphs and affordability status.
- Loadout preparation shows tier pips and colored stat deltas.
- Victory moment celebration includes a radial halo and sparkle burst.
- Co-op dungeon browser received minor layout improvements.

### Other improvements

- Home activity focus updated with additional context.
- World chapter navigator layout refinement.
- Smithing collection display improvements for clarity and density.
- Combat Power companion attribution capped at 18%, preventing Companion from dominating the build rating.
- Equipment tier memory system for tracking per-tier crafting context.
- Social notification state v1 migration prepared.
- Tool blueprint probabilities finalized at 3.5%, 1.4%, 0.7% tiers with a 0.10% floor; tool crafting awards up to +75% additional Smithing XP.
- Reinforced Timber and Bannerweave Cloth profession unlocks lowered to level 4.

## Explicitly not part of the 1.0.9 launch claim

- **Player Market:** pricing, custody, escrow, level thresholds and affordability work remains hidden and release-gated. There is no live trading UI or item transfer service.
- **World Bosses:** V10–V13 encounter, War Camp, Guard roles and role diagnostics remain a disabled prototype.
- **Dungeon Marks Shop:** catalogue, pricing and transactional foundations remain hard-off.
- **Arena and public Rankings:** both remain release-gated.
- **Ashlands:** world and Dungeon content remain In Development.
- **Frostfall event:** prepared but not claimed until its scheduled event window opens.
- Community Roadmap entries describe proposals; a vote count or roadmap stage does not mean the feature is playable.
