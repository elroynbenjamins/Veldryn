## In-game What's New — eight candidate highlights

**Title:** Beyond the Depths

1. **Equipment Rolls:** Newly forged gear can gain tier-appropriate bonus stats from Tier 1 onward, with existing equipment protected.
2. **Stackable Gathering Tools:** Equip up to four tool tiers per Mining, Woodcutting or Fishing skill for cumulative bonuses and hunt rarer blueprints.
3. **Dungeon Endurance:** Push through increasingly dangerous rooms and decide whether to face a boss after each seven-room checkpoint.
4. **Dungeon Weekly Quests:** Complete dedicated weekly Dungeon challenges for Expedition Marks, separate from your regular Quests.
5. **Friends & Guild Allies:** Choose eligible friends or guildmates for Solo/Echo Dungeons, with balanced power and required team roles.
6. **Boons & Artifacts:** Discover more temporary bonuses, artifact choices and strategic rewards as you explore deeper Dungeon rooms.
7. **Everyday Progress:** Enjoy Supply Journey boosts, Companion XP banking, extra Class Challenges and clearer Cooking XP.
8. **A More Polished Realm:** Experience new audio, smoother combat layouts, Guild alerts and better interface details.

**Publication date:** Only set when the verified 1.0.11 rollout actually happens. Do not publish draft entries.
