# VELDRYN v21 — Sunscar Gameplay Completion

Sunscar remains Region II, approximately Lv25–45. v20 established its five zones, 16 normal enemies, four bosses, 12 resources, ten-story questline, four Echo conditions, relic hooks, collectibles and three co-op dungeons. v21 completes the region's non-gear gameplay layer.

## Added in v21
- 8 authored regional side quests spread across all five zones.
- 5 repeatable regional activities with authoritative expected-time/difficulty metadata for social contribution scoring.
- 6 regional achievements and a 16-entry Sunscar Monster collection book.
- 3 season/weather interaction rules: Clear Heat, Glasswind and Sandstorm.
- 3 broad regional Contract templates: Combat, Skilling and Mixed. They are tags/templates, not one exact quest dependency.
- 4 Sand Tyrant mastery tiers focused on prestige, cosmetics and collectible progress rather than permanent raw power.
- Region Journal hooks so the player can see Story, Side Quests, Echoes, Co-op, Collection and Boss Mastery in one completion view.

## Difficulty philosophy
Side quests should typically take roughly 5–15 minutes of focused play or overlap normal idle/skilling actions. They are not mandatory grind walls. Regional Contract templates represent approximately 70–85 standardized minutes before Party/Guild scaling and reuse the existing effort-normalization engine.

## No new economy system
v21 does not add regional reputation, regional tokens or a player Market. Rewards reuse Gold, existing materials, cosmetics, account progression hooks, relic pity and existing collectible unlock paths.

## Equipment boundary
Sunscar weapons/armor/set pieces remain intentionally unauthored. Existing spreadsheet Sunscar gear is still draft/do-not-implement. The generic v20 set-bonus engine remains available for the later equipment rework.
