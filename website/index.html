◆ Chapter I · Your story

Become the hero
you choose to be.

From the first swing of a blade to a guild's greatest victory, every step is yours to take.

01 / Combat

Master your class.

Nine classes. Three combat roles. Create a hero, develop your skills and shape your own build.

02 / Professions

Craft your destiny.

Gather resources, refine materials and forge equipment. Turn time and skill into meaningful progress.

03 / Dungeons

Stand together.

Build a party of four and take on dungeon encounters, powerful enemies and rewarding challenges.

04 / Collection

Make it yours.

Discover companions, pets and collectible cosmetics. Build a collection that reflects your journey.

05 / Guilds

Find your people.

Make friends, contribute to guild projects and share victories with adventurers across the realm.

06 / Idle progression

Grow at your pace.

Set your next activity, step away and return to your rewards within your offline reserve.

◆ Before you begin

Adventurer's handbook.

Do I need an account to start?

You can begin as a guest. Secure your account later in the in-game account settings to help protect your progress.

Can I play at my own pace?

Yes. Veldryn is designed around idle activities and offline progression, subject to your in-game offline reserve.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Veldryn includes friends, guilds, parties and cooperative dungeons. Some features unlock as you progress.

Where can I get support or report a bug?

Join the official Veldryn Discord for community help and feedback. For privacy and account-related requests, see the privacy policy.
