## In-game What's New — eight highlights

1. **Equipment Rolls:** New T1–T5 gear can roll class-neutral bonus stats; existing items keep their progress.
2. **Gathering Tools:** Stack four tool tiers and forge rarity, quality and one Speed or Rare Find bonus per tool.
3. **Dungeon Endurance:** Face escalating rooms and choose a boss at every seven-room checkpoint.
4. **Dungeon Weeklies & Echoes:** Earn Expedition Marks from weekly challenges and recruit eligible friend or guild Echo allies.
5. **Deeper Dungeons:** Discover more boons, artifacts and clearer enemy previews and room choices.
6. **Social Name & Showcases:** Choose one account-wide name and present each hero's own showcase.
7. **Everyday Progress:** Enjoy Supply Journey boosts, Companion XP banking, Cooking XP previews and extra Class Challenges.
8. **Cleaner Adventures:** Improved crafting, inventory, gear comparisons, stable Character Roster, Rewards Inbox and audio controls.

## Included features and boundaries

- **Account-wide Social Name & hero showcases:** One chosen Social Name follows the account rather than changing when the active character switches. Per-character public showcase browsing is separate from account privacy and identity. Names must be unique, use the supported 2–20 character input policy and obey a 30-day change cooldown. All seven canonical production Social Name migrations were applied on 22 November; live permission/RLS, rollback-only identity and legacy compatibility regressions passed. Production EAS opt-in is ON and still requires matching production channel/project. Signed 1.0.11 Android account-switch, guest/Google, localization, privacy and Play-track QA remain necessary.
- **Crafted gear:** New T1–T5 accessible equipment has class-neutral bonus rolls. The underlying forge supports future tiers, but do not advertise locked T6–T9. Legacy items and receipts remain valid. This is not the full Equipment 2.0 system.
- **Gathering:** Four stacking gathering-tool tiers, each newly crafted tool with rarity, 0–100 quality and exactly one Speed (+2–8%) or Rare Find (+2–5%) bonus; effective 80% action-time reduction cap. Only gathering activities with eligible rare drops benefit from Rare Find.
- **Dungeon Endurance:** Escalating rooms and boss choices at each seven-room checkpoint; expanded boons/artifacts and clearer route previews. Dungeon weekly quests award Expedition Marks, and eligible friend/Guild Echo allies can fill Solo Dungeon roles. Production service authorization and real-client tests remain required.
- **Progression & interface:** Supply Journey/Boost Vault, Companion XP banking, Cooking XP previews, Class Challenges, compact Skills Navigator and recipes, Gear Storage, always-visible equipment comparisons and transfer/sell/salvage actions, compact Character Roster with stable creation order, and Rewards Inbox/Guild improvements.
- **Audio:** Region/activity sound controls and accepted original Town/Asterfall tracks. Do not promise additional unapproved replacement music.

## Excluded from the 1.0.11 public promise

Do not announce unfinished World Bosses, Party Events, Player Market, Guild PvE, public Rankings, or full Combat/Equipment/Classes/Skills 2.0. Social Name is a **1.0.11 feature**, not a 2.0 preview.
